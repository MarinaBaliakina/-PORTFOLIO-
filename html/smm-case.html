Social Media Management

Humber Polytechnic

From May 2025 to May 2026, I managed Humber Polytechnic's international social media presence across EMEA, Turkish, and Farsi accounts with student-focused storytelling, trend-aware reels, testimonials, and localized content built to increase awareness and qualified inquiries.

Strategy & Results

Goal

Increase awareness of Humber Polytechnic in EMEA regions and boost qualified student inquiries.

Strategy

The strategy focused on making Humber’s international content feel relatable, useful, and relevant to prospective students. I created content around student life, Canadian experiences, campus resources, trends, and Humber’s unique benefits to help the accounts connect with a broader student audience and make them imagine themselves at Humber.

Outcome

Views per post 100-300→2,000-4,000
Likes per post 0-4→20-120

Student Testimonials

Authentic student testimonials for Humber International EMEA, sharing personal experiences and success stories.

Goal

Build trust and credibility by showcasing real student voices and their journeys at Humber.

Strategy

Conduct and film interviews with students from EMEA regions, highlighting their experiences, challenges, and successes. Focus on questions that allow students' personalities to come through naturally, to keep it relatable and authentic.

Informational Carousels

Educational carousel content designed to simplify admissions, programs, deadlines, and student resources for prospective Humber International EMEA students.

Goal

Make important application and student-life information easier to understand, save, and revisit during the decision-making process.

Strategy

Break complex information into clear slide-by-slide narratives using concise copy, visual hierarchy, and student-friendly language that feels accessible on Instagram.

Outcome

Created practical, saveable resources that supported student inquiries, reduced confusion around key processes, and gave the account more evergreen informational content.
