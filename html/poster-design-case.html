PSA Campaign

Sex Trafficking Awareness PSA

A PSA campaign using photography and graphic design to communicate a message about human trafficking, focusing on how the issue can affect people within everyday communities.

01

PSA Concept

This campaign explores human trafficking awareness through a series of advertisements built around familiar relationships: classmate, neighbour, and daughter.

Goal: Create a campaign that makes the issue feel direct while communicating the seriousness of the topic through a clear visual message.

Process: Researched human trafficking statistics and discovered the estimated $280,000 value associated with a victim, which became the foundation for the campaign’s visual concept. Created studio photography for each advertisement, then developed the final layouts through image editing, typography and call to action placement.

Result: A PSA campaign developed into three graphics: a street banner, magazine page, and website ad placement.

02

Final Formats

The PSA was adapted into three formats so the campaign could stay recognizable across both physical and digital environments.

03

Campaign Mockups

The mockups show how the PSA system would appear in real-world placements.

04

Project Overview

The final campaign translates one awareness message across different print and digital formats.

Tools Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign
Duration 6 weeks
Deliverable Digital and print-ready PSA materials
