Video & Branding Project

Planty

A complete brand identity for Planty, an innovative plant-sitting service. Developed strategy, branding assets and identity, and a final pitch video from concept to launch with Maria Gomez.

01

Group Project

Planty was developed as a collaborative brand and video project. The concept, brand direction, and core ideas were shaped together, while the final deliverables were divided by each person’s production strengths.

Marina

Brand assets and video production

  • Logo design
  • Filming and video editing
  • Business card and merch applications

Maria

Digital applications and video role

  • Website, app, and Facebook page
  • Actor and voice over
  • Process infographic in the video
02

Brand Strategy

Established Planty's market position as a reliable, modern plant care service targeting busy professionals. Developed brand voice, core values, and messaging framework.

03

Logo Sketches

Explored early logo directions through hand-drawn paper sketches, testing leaf shapes, lettering, and simple plant-care symbols before moving into digital refinement.

04

Digital Applications

Assisted Maria in designing a responsive Facebook page, website, and mobile app interfaces showcasing services, pricing, and customer testimonials.

05

Marketing & Collateral

Created brand assets, including business cards, packaging, and garden-related products.

06

Pitch Video

Professional video production showcasing Planty's brand story, service offering, and value proposition. Filmed and edited the final result.

07

Project Overview

The final project brought together brand strategy, identity design, digital mockups, physical collateral, and a pitch video for a plant-sitting service concept.

Tools Illustrator, Canva, Premiere Pro
Duration 12 weeks
Deliverable Brand identity, collateral, digital mockups, and pitch video
