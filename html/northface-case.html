The North Face Projection Mapping

A motion design concept visualizing The North Face's all-season versatility through projection mapping. Animated seasonal transitions reveal brand adaptability across environments, climates, and lifestyles.

01

Group Project

This project was created collaboratively with Maria Gomez. We developed the projection mapping concept together and shared the final mapping work, while dividing the illustration, animation, and audio tasks across the production.

Marina

Illustration and core motion

  • Sketches and concept visuals
  • Illustrator assets for all seasons
  • Main season-change animation

Maria

Animation details and audio

  • Snow, sun movement, and flowers
  • Final animation touches
  • Sound design
02

Concept & Strategy

Together with Maria Gomez, developed a projection mapping concept that showcases The North Face's ability to thrive in any environment. The design uses four seasonal transitions to tell the brand story, with each season revealing a distinct color palette and atmosphere while progressively building the iconic quarter-circle logo.

03

Illustration & Visual Design

Created detailed seasonal illustrations and vector assets for the full scene across spring, summer, fall, and winter. Each environment was built to support the final logo reveal.

04

Animation & Motion Design

Animated the major seasonal transitions with colour changes and nature assets appearing and disappearing. Maria added final animation details, including snow, sun movement, and flowers.

05

Projection Mapping

Mapped the animation onto a physical quarter-circle foam object together, creating an immersive brand experience. The final frame completes the full logo before switching back to the next season and repeating the loop.

06

Production Workflow

The project moved from vector illustration to motion design, sound, and projection mapping, combining visual asset creation with technical output for the final physical installation.

AiAdobe Illustrator

Created detailed seasonal illustrations and visual assets with vibrant colour palettes.

AeAdobe After Effects

Animated transitions between seasons and built complex motion graphics sequences.

AuAdobe Audition

Edited and synchronized sound design to enhance visual transitions and emotional impact.

TDTouchDesigner

Mapped animations onto the 3D logo structure and controlled the final projection output.

07

Project Overview

The final deliverable was a projection mapping motion piece showing The North Face as an adaptable all-season brand.

Tools Illustrator, After Effects, Audition, TouchDesigner
Duration 8 weeks
