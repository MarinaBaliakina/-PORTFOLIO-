Humber Hawks Nation

From September 2025 to May 2026, I worked as a photographer for Humber Hawks Nation, an official on-campus support club for varsity sports at Humber Polytechnic. Additional duties included social media and web management, turning games and events into polished photo and video content.

Event Recap & Promotion

Short-form social coverage built around game energy, athlete moments, and event recaps for Humber Hawks Nation.

Goal

Keep the athletics audience engaged beyond game day with content that felt current, energetic, and easy to share.

Strategy

Edit fast-paced reels from games, promotions, and behind-the-scenes moments while keeping the focus on athletes and fan energy.

Outcome

Built a repeatable social content format that made event coverage feel current, energetic, and easy to revisit.

Photography

Game & Event Photography

Delivered sports and event photography for use across social media, the athletics website, and printed materials. The work focused on athlete expression, movement, team culture, and moments that could support multiple platforms after each event.

Website Management

Photo-Led Web Updates

Created the photo website from scratch based on a management request to find a cost-effective way to share images with fans and grow engagement. I managed the site by uploading event and game photos weekly, helping sports content move from event coverage into a lasting digital home where fans and athletes could revisit highlights, coverage, and team moments.
