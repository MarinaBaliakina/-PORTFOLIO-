Vinyl Cover Design

A music packaging project featuring two vinyl cover concepts developed for different artists and genres. The project includes album artwork, packaging design, and supporting promotional materials.

Project Structure

Two Cover Directions

This project presents two vinyl cover designs created for different musical identities. Oranged is a real musician, my brother, creating electronic music, while Savannah Wilder is a fictional character in country-inspired style style.

01 Oranged

02 Savannah
ORANGED
01

Artist & Concept

Oranged was created for my brother, who is a musician. The idea was to build a personal cover direction around original photography I took of him, making the artwork feel connected to the artist instead of using generic music imagery.

02

Oranged Moodboard

Built a visual moodboard to define the style centered around electronic music, marker lines, hand-drawn elements on top of photography.

03

Oranged Sketches

Explored early composition ideas for the Oranged cover, testing how the portrait, title placement, and album-cover structure could work together before moving into the final layout.

04

Oranged Insert

Designed an insert as a supporting packaging piece, with a made-up song lyrics since the artist's songs don't have words, to illustrate how lyrics could be integrated into the design.

05

Oranged Final Mockups

The final Oranged design was printed using a layout for folding an actual vinyl cover and insert. Displaying the uses original photography, texture effects, and marker line text font.

06

Artist Website

I also coded a simple website concept for selling his merch and songs, extending the music packaging direction into a digital world.

SAVANNAH
01

Artist & Concept

Savannah was developed as a country-inspired record cover concept. The idea was to lean into western mood, warm tones, and a strong genre atmosphere while keeping the cover clean enough to work as music packaging.

02

Savannah Moodboard

Gathered country-inspired references to shape the western atmosphere, colour direction, and visual language for the second cover.

03

Savannah Sketches

Developed the Savannah concept through rough sketches, focusing on how country references, space, and cover hierarchy could create a clear base for the final design.

04

Savannah Process

The process video shows the hand-drawn process of drawing the female silhouette for the Savannah cover in Photoshop.

05

Savannah Final Mockups

The final Savannah Wilder's "Letter to Nowhere" cover design displayed on a vinyl record mockups.

06

Project Overview

The final project presents two record cover concepts with distinct art directions, moving from sketches and mood references into polished vinyl packaging mockups.

Tools Photoshop, Illustrator, photography
Duration 7 weeks
Deliverable Two vinyl cover, an insert, and website designs
