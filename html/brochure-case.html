TORONTO SCHOOL OF ART BROCHURE
01

Process & Result

Designed a three-fold brochure for the Toronto School of Art that introduces the school, its programs, and course offerings through a multi-page printed publication.

02

Printed Mockup

The mockup presents the brochure as a folded print piece, showing the final layout, typography, imagery, and page structure.

03

Brochure Overview

The final direction turns the brochure into a more polished communication piece with clearer hierarchy, better pacing, and a layout system that supports arts education content.

Tools InDesign, Photoshop
Duration 2 weeks
Deliverable Print-ready brochure design
NONVIOLENT COMMUNICATION BOOK COVER
01

Original Cover to Redesign

This project redesigns the cover of Nonviolent Communication by Marshall Rosenberg. The original cover served as the starting point for developing a calmer visual direction that reflects empathy, listening, and intentional communication.

02

Printed Mockup

The mockup shows the redesigned cover applied to a printed book.

03

Book Cover Overview

The final direction reframes the book with a quieter visual system, using composition and typography to support the subject instead of overpowering it.

Tools Photoshop, Illustrator
Duration 4 weeks
Deliverable Book cover redesign and mockups
